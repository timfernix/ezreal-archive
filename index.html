About this project

Project

Ezreal Media Archive is a searchable gallery that helps fans browse official Ezreal assets.

Created by timfernix. Contributions are welcome on GitHub.

Favorites & cookies

Your favorites are stored in a cookie in your browser (no account, nothing is sent to a server). If you delete your cookies, your favorites are gone as well.
